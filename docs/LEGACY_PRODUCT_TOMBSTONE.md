# Legacy product tombstone

GlamGenius previously explored an appearance, style, and wardrobe product.
That direction is abandoned. Historical references may remain in Git history or
immutable database migrations; they are not product authority.

Wardrobe cataloguing, Style Me, style quiz, colour analysis, virtual try-on,
general outfit generation, salon directory/booking, unrestricted AI chat, and
public social are permanently rejected. See `PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md`. Do not
reconstruct or revive them without explicit founder instruction.
