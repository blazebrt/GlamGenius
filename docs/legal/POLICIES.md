# Public User Policies (DRAFTS)

> **DISCLAIMER:** These are drafts and must not be presented as legal advice. The owner must review and approve the wording before publishing.

## Privacy Policy
We collect minimal personal data necessary to provide personalized beauty and wellness routines.
- **Camera/Photos**: Processed strictly for AI analysis (Gemini). Raw photos are NOT logged or stored beyond the temporary processing window.
- **Third parties**: We do not sell data. We share anonymized data with our AI provider (Google Gemini) for inference only.
- **Retention**: Data is kept as long as your account is active.

## Terms of Service
By using GlamGenius, you agree to our beta terms. The app is provided "as is". We reserve the right to limit AI features to prevent abuse.

## Data-retention policy
- Active accounts: Indefinite retention.
- Deleted accounts: Hard deletion of all relational data within 30 days. Media files in Supabase Storage are purged immediately upon deletion request via background workers.

## Account-deletion policy
Users can delete their account directly within the app (Settings -> Account -> Delete Account). The deletion is permanent, irreversible, and executed by our async deletion worker.

## AI-assisted recommendation disclosure
GlamGenius uses Google Gemini AI to analyze your features and recommend styles/products. AI recommendations are not flawless and should be reviewed with your own judgment.

## Photo-processing disclosure
Your photos are securely transmitted to Google's Gemini models for real-time analysis. We do not retain these photos after the session ends unless explicitly saved by you to your private gallery.

## Supplement and wellness disclaimer
Any wellness or supplement advice generated by the AI is for informational purposes only and is not medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional.

## Age/minor-use policy
You must be 13 years or older to use GlamGenius.
